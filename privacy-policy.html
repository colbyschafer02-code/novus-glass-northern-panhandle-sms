SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Novus Glass of Northern Panhandle TX (“Novus Glass,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by Panhandle Paints and Mechanical LLC, respects your privacy. This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information in connection with our text-message communications program.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, request an estimate, schedule service, receive automotive glass services, complete a customer form, or otherwise interact with us, we may collect information such as your name, mobile phone number, email address, vehicle and service information, appointment details, and information you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to inquiries, provide estimates, schedule and confirm appointments, provide repair or replacement updates, send service reminders, provide customer support, follow up regarding requested or completed services, and send promotional communications when you have provided the required consent.

SMS and Mobile Information

If you opt in to receive text messages from Novus Glass of Northern Panhandle TX, your mobile number may be used to send customer-service, appointment, estimate, service-update, reminder, follow-up, and other messages for which you have provided consent.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not sell, rent, or transfer your mobile phone number or SMS consent information to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency may vary based on your interactions and services with Novus Glass of Northern Panhandle TX. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us using the information below.

Service Providers

We may use service providers that help us operate our business and deliver requested communications. Such providers may process information as necessary to perform services on our behalf. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in information are not provided to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may stop receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request help by replying HELP. You may also contact us directly regarding your information or communications preferences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
